# 5. Business findings

The comparisons cover January and February of each year. The 2024 raw period includes February 29. The Power BI YoY KPI pairs dates by day and month and drops unmatched leap days. Results describe the BTS reporting population and vary with carrier, airport and route mix.

| Finding | Evidence | Recommended operational follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| The two-month average masks a January–February reversal | In January, arrival OTP rose from 75.94% in 2024 to 81.21% in 2025. February fell from 84.08% to 79.23%. Overall OTP increased from 79.97% to 80.25%, and cancellation rate increased from 2.19% to 2.27%. | Review February by date, reporting carrier, directional route and station. Compare with January and control for changes in mix before attributing the change. |
| Peer comparisons depend on operating mix | In Jan–Feb 2025, HA had 84.65% arrival OTP on 12,523 comparable arrivals, WN had 84.16% on 201,626, and OH had 69.42% on 36,616. All clear the default volume threshold. | Compare on shared or comparable routes and periods. Use rank and volume to choose where to review local operating procedures. |
| Direction changes the priority on the BOS–DCA pair | BOS → DCA logged 34,202 late-arrival minutes and 68.84% OTP on 1,425 comparable arrivals. DCA → BOS logged 25,093 minutes and 77.15% OTP on 1,427 arrivals. | Start with the BOS → DCA departure banks and dates with the largest delay minutes. Review the linked flight records with station and airline teams. |
| Cancellation counts and attributed late minutes highlight different mechanisms | In Jan–Feb 2025, 19,753 of 23,717 reported cancellations (83.29%) were weather-coded. Among the 14.36M minutes on eligible 15+ late arrivals, late-arriving aircraft accounted for 5.15M minutes and air carrier for 5.02M minutes. | Review weather-coded cancellation dates and station contingency plans. Review late-aircraft and carrier-attributed delays alongside internal turnaround and aircraft-rotation records. |

The attribution is reported by carriers under BTS rules. These records support comparisons of reported disruption categories and flight outcomes. Confirm operational root causes with station records and internal event data. The public extract has no cost, margin, compensation, passenger-connection, or revenue fields.

Sources [BTS public source](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/PREZIP/), [BTS delay and cancellation categories](https://www.bts.gov/topics/airlines-and-airports/understanding-reporting-causes-flight-delays-and-cancellations), [Monthly reconciliation](monthly-reconciliation.csv).
